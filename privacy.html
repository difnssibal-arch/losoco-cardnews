Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Overview

losoco-cardnews ("the Service") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize the Service via TikTok Login Kit, we collect the following information from TikTok's API:

  • TikTok Open ID (unique account identifier)
  • Display name and avatar URL
  • Access token and refresh token (used solely to post content on your behalf)

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any other personal data beyond what TikTok's API provides.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account
  • Publish photo card content to your TikTok account via the Content Posting API
  • Maintain session continuity using stored tokens

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties.

4. Data Storage

Access tokens are stored locally in a configuration file on the operator's device. No user data is stored on external servers or databases. Tokens are only used for the purpose of publishing content as authorized by the user.

5. TikTok Data

This Service uses TikTok's official API. Your use of TikTok's platform is also governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy, available at tiktok.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your access token only as long as necessary to operate the Service. You may revoke access at any time by disconnecting the app from your TikTok account settings, after which all stored tokens become invalid.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke the Service's access to your TikTok account at any time
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your account
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions or concerns

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
